Classification Standards

Explore Waste Categories

Understanding the 8 core waste streams prevents recycling contamination and protects sanitation teams.

🌱 Green Bin

Wet / Organic Waste

Kitchen scraps, fruit and vegetable peels, coffee grounds, and garden trimmings that decompose naturally.

Examples: Fruit peels, tea bags, egg shells, vegetable cut-offs
📦 Blue Bin

Paper & Cardboard

Clean, dry paper, corrugated shipping boxes, newspapers, and unsoiled office cartons.

Examples: Shipping boxes, newspapers, magazines, notebooks
🧴 Yellow Bin

Plastic Waste

Rigid plastics like PET bottles (#1) and HDPE jugs (#2). Clean, rinse, and crush before disposal.

Examples: Water bottles, shampoo jugs, plastic food tubs
🍾 Cyan Bin

Glass Waste

Container glass is 100% infinitely recyclable. Remove metal or plastic lids and rinse out remnants.

Examples: Sauce jars, beverage bottles, olive oil containers
🥫 Grey Bin

Metal Waste

Aluminum beverage cans, steel food tins, clean foil trays, and metal bottle lids.

Examples: Soda cans, tomato paste tins, clean foil
📱 Purple Bin

Electronic Waste (E-Waste)

Contains recoverable precious metals (gold, copper) alongside heavy metals. Never mix in household bins.

Examples: Old phones, chargers, computer cables, tablets
🗑️ Black Bin

General / Non-Recyclable

Materials contaminated by food oils, composites, multi-layer foil chip wrappers, or non-recyclable ceramics.

Examples: Greasy pizza boxes, potato chip packets, styrofoam
⚠️ Red Bin

Hazardous / Special

Batteries, solvents, paints, and flammables that risk groundwater contamination and truck fires.

Examples: Lithium batteries, paint cans, chemical solvents, fluorescent tubes

Practical Knowledge

Essential Recycling Guidelines

Avoid "wishcycling" — putting items in recycling hoping they get recycled. Follow these 6 proven rules.

1 💧

Clean & Dry Before Tossing

Food residues, sauces, and liquids ruin paper fibers and contaminate plastic batches. Give containers a quick scrape or light rinse and let them dry.

2 🍕

The Greasy Box Rule

Grease from pizzas or takeout cannot be separated from paper during the water-based pulping process. Tear off the clean lid to recycle, and put greasy bottoms in general waste or compost.

3 🔋

Never Bin Batteries

Lithium and alkaline batteries can ignite garbage trucks and sorting plants when compacted. Always drop them off at dedicated battery collection points or supermarket return kiosks.

4 🏷️

Look for Resin Codes (1–7)

Check the triangular chasing arrows with a number inside. Numbers 1 (PET) and 2 (HDPE) are widely recycled almost everywhere, while numbers 3–7 often require specialized facilities.

5 🔌

E-Waste Contains Treasures

Old cables, cracked phones, and dead laptops contain precious copper, silver, and rare earths. Taking them to certified e-waste drives prevents hazardous heavy metals from polluting groundwater.

6 📦

Flatten All Cardboard

Leaving boxes unflattened wastes up to 70% of collection bin space, causing overflow and unnecessary collection truck trips. Break down and flatten every shipping box.
